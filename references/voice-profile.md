# Voice Profile

Capture a client's voice **once**, at package kickoff, and reuse it across every page and blog. This is what makes a 40-page site plus twelve months of blogs sound like one consistent business instead of a dozen separate AI sessions. It is a client-facing artifact (the WPC can confirm it with the client) and a drafter-facing one (every draft references it).

## When to build it

At the start of a New Content Package, right after ingesting the intake. Refresh it if the client rebrands or gives new direction. For blog production and refreshes on an existing client, reuse the profile already on file.

## Sources to pull from (in priority order)

1. **The intake + discovery interview** — tagline, differentiators, how the owner describes the work, anything they explicitly want said or avoided.
2. **Their best existing copy** — a page or About section they're proud of shows their natural register.
3. **Their Google/Yelp reviews** — the sleeper source. Customers describe the business in the exact words prospects search and relate to. Mine reviews for recurring phrases, the outcomes customers care about, and the tone people use about them.
4. **The vertical norms** — how trustworthy operators in this trade talk (plain and competent for trades; warmer and more credential-forward for medical/wellness; precise for legal).

## What the profile contains

Keep it short — a page at most. Capture:
- **Persona:** who is "speaking" (e.g., a locally owned, owner-operated fence builder who treats your property like his own).
- **Tone words:** 3–5 adjectives (e.g., honest, direct, neighborly, competent).
- **Reading level:** default 8th–9th grade; note any justified exception.
- **Signature phrases / tagline:** real lines to echo (e.g., "Honesty. Integrity. Quality Craftsmanship.").
- **Proof to lean on:** the real E-E-A-T signals (years, licenses, guarantees, review presence) — each marked `⚑ VERIFY` until confirmed.
- **Do-not-say list:** unverifiable superlatives ("#1 in the area"), banned phrases, anything the client dislikes.
- **Audience:** who they're talking to (demographics, primary need, secondary needs).

## How it's used downstream

- Every page and blog draft opens by loading the profile, then applies `writing-guidelines.md` on top of it.
- The QA rubric checks output against the profile (voice consistency, do-not-say list, proof used).
- In the GAS pipeline, the profile is stored once per client and passed into every drafting call (see `automation-gas.md`).

## Worked example (abridged — Masters Fence Co)

- **Persona:** Locally owned, owner-operated fence builder (Jason Masters) who treats your property like his own.
- **Tone:** Honest, direct, neighborly, competent. Confidence without hype.
- **Reading level:** 8th grade.
- **Signature:** "Honesty. Integrity. Quality Craftsmanship." · free demolition + haul-away of the old fence with a new install.
- **Proof (⚑ VERIFY):** locally owned; strong reviews on Yelp, HomeAdvisor, Angi, BBB; 24/7 by phone/email; free quotes.
- **Do not say:** "#1 contractor in the area" (unverifiable superlative) — convert to real proof.
- **Audience:** Affluent homeowners 35+ in the 35242 corridor who value privacy, security, curb appeal, and craftsmanship; secondary pool owners and HOA/commercial.
